### 👨‍💻 About Me

I'm a Software Engineer and CS student (Bahria University, top 10%) who builds production SaaS products end-to-end — architecture, backend, frontend, and the business behind them. I run **[Sajomnix](https://www.sajomnix.dev/)**, a software house offering full-stack development, cybersecurity/pen-testing, and DevOps, and I'm currently deep in the weeds on:

- 🏥 **MediBook** — a Doctor Appointment SaaS (Next.js + NestJS) with Stripe/JazzCash/EasyPaisa payments, WebRTC video consultations, and RBAC-hardened auth
- 🌾 **Kisan Ka Pakistan** — a full-stack agricultural mandi platform (NestJS · Prisma · PostgreSQL · Next.js) with Redis caching, price aggregation, and farmer ledgers, live at [kisan-ka-pakistan.vercel.app](https://kisan-ka-pakistan.vercel.app)
- 📰 **Pakistan.fm** — a Pakistani blogging & knowledge platform with date-driven theming and a quiz/certificate system
- 💌 **CardCraft** — a digital invitation e-commerce site for weddings & events

I like taking systems apart to understand *why* they work — connection pooling, cache-aside patterns, queue architecture — not just shipping features.
